⚡ How LightCoach Works
🎯 What is this?
LightCoach is a paper trading simulator — you practice buying and selling real cryptocurrencies using virtual money. No real funds are ever used. It's designed to help you learn how crypto trading works before risking real money.
💼 Primary vs ✨ Dream Accounts
Primary Account — set to match your real investment amount so the experience feels realistic.

Dream Account — unlimited play money. Trade like a billionaire with no consequences. Toggle between them at the top of the screen.
📱 How to navigate
📊 Dashboard — Your portfolio, goals, and achievements.
🌐 Markets — Browse live prices for 100+ coins.
⚡ Trade — Search for a coin and tap it to buy or sell.
📓 Notes — Write down strategies and trading ideas.
🎓 Coach — AI trading coach. Subscribe with LCAI to unlock.
🛒 How to make your first trade
1. Tap the ⚡ Trade tab
2. Search for a coin (e.g. Bitcoin)
3. Tap the coin card
4. Enter an amount and tap Buy
5. Your holdings will appear in the Dashboard
🏅 Ranks & Achievements
Make trades to level up from 🐟 Guppy → 📊 Trader → 🦈 Shark → 💰 Whale → 🌟 Legend. Unlock achievement badges for milestones like diversifying your portfolio or turning a profit.
⚠️ Important: LightCoach is for educational purposes only. Nothing here is financial advice. All prices are real but all trades are simulated — no real money is ever used or at risk. The service may experience downtime. No refunds apply to any future paid features.
